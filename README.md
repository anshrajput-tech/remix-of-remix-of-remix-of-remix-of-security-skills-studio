# Remix of Remix of Remix of Remix of Security Skills Studio

Build a complete website for eSecurityIN, a cybersecurity training institute.

Website references


Original content website: https://eSecurity.com

1. Overall Design Direction

Take inspiration from Craw Security's SIMPLE WEBSITE STRUCTURE and EASY NAVIGATION.

I specifically like the straightforward approach:

Simple navigation bar.

Clearly organised information.

Easy-to-find courses.

Direct headings and descriptions.

Structured content sections.

Professional institute presentation.

Simple enquiry options.

Information presented without unnecessary decoration.

However, DO NOT replicate its exact design.

Create an original visual identity for eSecurityIN.

Theme: Light and Clean

Use:

White backgrounds.

Very light grey and subtle blue section backgrounds.

Deep navy blue for headings.

Royal blue for primary buttons.

Subtle cyan accents.

Dark grey body text.

Use consistent typography, comfortable spacing, clean layouts and simple cards.

The website should feel like a professional educational institute, not a flashy technology startup.

Avoid:

Excessive animations.

Complex scrolling effects.

Too many gradients.

Huge decorative illustrations.

Neon colours.

Unnecessary floating elements.

Excessively rounded cards.

Overcrowded sections.

Complicated layouts.

Prioritise simplicity, readability and usability.

2. Content Preservation – Extremely Important

The existing eSecurityIN website is the ONLY primary source for the institute's actual information.

Inspect all accessible pages and preserve:

About Us information.

All existing courses.

Course descriptions.

Training programs.

Course curriculum.

Course duration.

Certifications.

Faculty details.

Services.

Institute information.

Infrastructure.

Student testimonials.

Achievements.

Contact details.

Address.

Other important information.

Do not remove important content.

Do not invent courses, certifications, trainers, achievements, placement statistics or testimonials.

You may improve the formatting, grammar and organisation of existing content without changing its meaning.

If some original pages cannot be accessed, identify the missing information rather than inventing it.

Keep the hero layout straightforward with text on one side and a photograph on the other.

About eSecurityIN

A concise introduction to the institute using the original website's content.

Use one genuine institute photograph alongside the text.

Add a Read More button linking to the About page.

Our Training Programs

Display all existing courses from eSecurityIN.

Use a simple, consistent grid of course cards.

Each card should contain:

Course name.

Use a simple two-column layout with relevant photographs or illustrations.

4. Individual Pages

Create dedicated pages for:

About Us.

All Courses.

Individual Course Details.

Offensive Security.

Defensive Security.

Our Institute.

Faculty.

Contact Us.

Keep every page consistent with the homepage design.

Preserve the original information and course details.

5. Image Usage

I will upload actual photographs of eSecurityIN.

Use these photographs throughout the website.

Prioritise:

Teachers teaching students using smartboards.

Offline classroom sessions.

Practical cybersecurity training.

Institute interiors.

Faculty photographs.

Do not replace these photographs with generic stock images.

Do not generate fictional photographs representing the actual institute.

Use cybersecurity icons and simple illustrations only where additional visuals are necessary.

6. Technical Requirements

Fully responsive.

Fast loading.

SEO-friendly.

Functional navigation.

Working enquiry forms.

Optimised photographs.

Clean reusable components.

Accessible typography.

Proper mobile navigation.

No broken links.

No non-functional buttons.

Keep animations minimal and subtle.

7. Final Design Instructions

The final website should have:

The simplicity and straightforward navigation of Craw Security.

An original design identity for eSecurityIN.

A clean white and light-blue visual theme.

Properly organised cybersecurity courses.

Equal emphasis on Offensive and Defensive Security.

Genuine institute photographs.

A strong presentation of offline smartboard classroom teaching.

Do not make the website unnecessarily complicated or visually overloaded.

Remember: SIMPLE STRUCTURE, UNIQUE DESIGN, LIGHT THEME, COMPLETE ESECURITYIN CONTENT.

The objective is to create a professional cybersecurity institute website that is easy for students and parents to understand, explore and enquire through.
